### Use Case: Warehouse Robot Pathfinding

### Problem Statement

As technology is progressing, more autonomous robots are being developed and used in warehouses for industries. The goal of this project is to design a pathfinding method for robots to travel to a destination around obstacles in a warehouse. The pathfinding is used in classical and quantum methods of calculations, each one having similar and different results.

### Why Quantum Computing?

The goal is to see if quantum computing can generate a more efficient path to the destination than classical methods of calculations can. The time it takes for each method to calculate is also compared.
